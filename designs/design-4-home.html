About us

Saudi expertise, backed by a global network

Masarat for Accreditation was founded in 2016 by Saudi expertise in the field of biosafety and cleanrooms, to help clients achieve the highest infection and contamination control standards and comply with accreditation bodies' requirements — including SFDA, CBAHI, WHO, CAP, CDC and many others.

We are specialized in the testing, commissioning and validation of clean rooms and sterile areas, and we deliver end-to-end solutions through our team of engineers and a wide network of national and international experts — backed by our partner MRC Solutions, with 35+ years building state-of-the-art cleanrooms.
