Air Quality Validation Testing

Air quality validation testing and accreditation to international standards (ISO 14644) — with consultancy advice, or full design and construction.

Areas we test

Operating rooms · drug-preparation rooms · isolation rooms · sterile areas (CSSD, IVF lab) · other areas requiring sterility or special airflow.

Tests

  • done Air flow rates (supply & extract)
  • done Containment & integrity
  • done HEPA filter leak test
  • done Pressure differential
  • done Airborne particle counting
  • done Smoke visualization

Optional: microbial counts · lighting · sound & vibration · gowning & protocol evaluation.
