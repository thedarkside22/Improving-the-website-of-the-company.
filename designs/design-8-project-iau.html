Overview

Containment and GLP, on one campus

Imam Abdulrahman Bin Faisal University required both a BSL-3 biocontainment laboratory and a Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) facility — each with the engineering and validation to meet its respective standard.

Masarat delivered both laboratories end-to-end — design and engineering, GRP construction and installation, airflow & pressure control, testing and validation, commissioning, handover and training. (Full project narrative is a placeholder pending sign-off.)
