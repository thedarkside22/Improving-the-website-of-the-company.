About Masarat for Accreditation

Saudi expertise in biosafety & cleanrooms, since 2016

We help clients achieve the highest infection and contamination control standards — and comply with accreditation bodies including SFDA, CBAHI, WHO, CAP and CDC.

Who we are

End-to-end solutions, under one accountable partner

Masarat for Accreditation was founded in 2016 by Saudi expertise in biosafety and cleanrooms. We specialize in the testing, commissioning and validation of clean rooms and sterile areas, and deliver end-to-end solutions — from consultancy through design, construction, testing, certification and training.

We pioneer in testing and maintaining cleanrooms, biosafety laboratories, controlled environments, biological safety cabinets, horizontal laminar airflow cabinets, isolators and fume hoods — all to meet international standards.
