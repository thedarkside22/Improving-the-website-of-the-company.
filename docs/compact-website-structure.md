# Masarat — Compact Website Structure

## Recommended main navigation

Keep only four navigation choices:

1. **Solutions**
2. **Projects**
3. **About**
4. **Contact**

Add a visually prominent **Request a Quote** button beside them.

Do not create separate “Products” and “Services” menus. The customer should find everything through one **Solutions** dropdown.

## Solutions dropdown

Use a wide, simple menu with three columns:

### Build & Engineer

- Modular Cleanrooms
- Biocontainment Laboratories
- Air Filtration & Environmental Monitoring

### Equip

- Laboratory Equipment
- Safety Cabinets & Hoods
- Laboratory Furniture

### Test & Maintain

- CQV & Cleanroom Testing
- Cabinet Certification & Preventive Maintenance

Every item should link directly to the relevant section of one combined **Solutions** page. This avoids unnecessary category and subcategory pages.

### Modular Cleanrooms

## Compact page structure

The whole website can be kept to five main pages:

1. **Home**
2. **Solutions** — all products and services on one filterable page
3. **Projects**
4. **About & Credentials**
5. **Contact / Request a Quote**

Individual product pages are unnecessary at the beginning. Each item on the Solutions page can open an expandable panel or quick-view drawer containing:

- A short explanation
- Three key criteria
- Main applications
- Brochure or credential download, when available
- Request a Quote button

## Solutions page layout

Use three filter tabs at the top:

**All · Build & Engineer · Equip · Test & Maintain**

Under the filters, show the eight cards above. Each card should contain:

- Clear title
- One-sentence purpose
- Three offerings or specifications
- **View Details** and **Request a Quote** actions

For equipment cards, open a quick-view panel instead of sending the visitor through several pages.

## Home page layout

Keep the home page short:

1. **Hero** — “Infection & Contamination Control Solutions” with Request a Quote
2. **What We Do** — the same three groups: Build, Equip, Test & Maintain
3. **Why Masarat** — standards-led, end-to-end delivery, Saudi expertise
4. **Selected Projects** — three or four projects only
5. **Standards & Credentials** — compact logo/credential strip with the NSF PDF link
6. **Final CTA** — discuss a project or request a site assessment

## NSF credential placement

Place the PDF in two locations only:

- A **View NSF Credential** link in About & Credentials
- A smaller link inside Cabinet Certification & Preventive Maintenance

Use the exact credential title from the PDF. Do not describe the whole company or every cabinet as “NSF certified” unless the document explicitly supports that claim.

## Mobile menu

On mobile, show:

- Solutions
  - Build & Engineer
  - Equip
  - Test & Maintain
- Projects
- About
- Contact
- Request a Quote

The three Solutions groups should behave as accordions. Keep all equipment types visible within two taps.
