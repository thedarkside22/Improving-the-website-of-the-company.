## 6. How we work — Key Project Phases & Milestones

A clear, professional delivery methodology (8 phases):

1. **Project Initiation** (kick-off meeting) — finalization of project
   requirements and scope; initial site assessment.
2. **Design Phase** — conceptual design development; detailed engineering and
   architectural design; approval of design by the client.
3. **Procurement Phase** — sourcing of materials and equipment; quality checks
   and preparation for shipping.
4. **Construction Phase** — site preparation and foundational work; assembly and
   installation of GRP panels and other structural components.
5. **Installation Phase** — installation of HVAC systems and cleanroom
   components; electrical and mechanical installations.
6. **Testing and Validation** — comprehensive testing of cleanroom and QC-lab
   components; validation to ensure ISO 14644 and GLP/GMP compliance.
7. **Commissioning and Handover** — final inspections and commissioning; handover
   of the completed facility to the client; training of client staff on
   operations and maintenance.
8. **Project Closure** — final documentation and project report; client feedback
   and project review.

## 7. Products - Modular Cleanroom (GRP panels)

**GRP (Glass Reinforced Polyester) Panels** provide a durable, molded
cleanroom paneling system. The advanced manufacturing technique and specific GRP
formulation create an impervious, smooth surface resistant to mechanical damage
and chemical attack. A thick, resin-rich Gel-coat surface offers high resistance
to scratches and abrasions, and is available in a wide range of colors with a
smooth, matte finish.

## 8. Service detail — Air Quality Validation Testing

Masarat for Accreditation can undertake **Air Quality Validation testing and
accreditation to international standards** (i.e. ISO 14644) and provide consultancy
advice — or even design and construction. Our experienced team of engineers and
technologists helps clients maintain operations to internationally approved
standards.

**Areas we can test:**
- Operating rooms
- Drug-preparation rooms
- Isolation rooms
- Sterile areas (e.g. CSSD, IVF lab)
- Other areas requiring sterility or special airflow specifications

**Tests / services:**
- **Air flow rates** — supply and extract
- **Containment & integrity tests** — ensure no leakage between clean rooms
- **HEPA filter leak test** — protects the working environment
- **Pressure differential test** — ensure correct flow of clean air
- **Airborne particle counting test** — determine air cleanliness & classification
- **Smoke visualization test** — verify proper airflow direction

**Optional tests / services:**
- Microbial counts
- Lighting tests
- Sound and vibration tests
- Evaluation of gowning and operational protocols

*Standards referenced on this service: ISO 14644-1:2015 / 14644-2:2015, USP.*

## 9. Service detail — Biological Safety Cabinet (BSC) Testing & Certification

**Why certify your cabinets? — to provide safety for:**
- Patient / product protection
- Personnel protection
- The environment
- Meeting the requirements of accreditation bodies (e.g. JCIA, CBAHI)

**Tests / services:**
- **Inflow air velocity test** — personnel protection
- **Downflow air velocity test** — product protection
- **HEPA filter leak test** — protects working agents and the room environment
- **Smoke visualization test** — verifies proper airflow direction
- **Site installation test** — verifies alarm setting and calibration
- **Light intensity test** — sufficient light to prevent operator fatigue
- **Vibration test** — ensures the product is not damaged by vibration
- **Electrical safety test** — ensures the unit is electrically safe for workers

*Standards/bodies referenced on this service: NSF, CBAHI.*
