# Masarat Website Product & Service Catalog Blueprint

Do not place every item under a single **Products** heading. Masarat offers three different things: engineered solutions, physical products, and technical services. Keeping them separate will make the website clearer and more credible.

### Primary navigation

1. **Solutions**
   - Modular Cleanrooms
   - Biocontainment Laboratories (BSL-3 & BSL-4)
   - Environmental Monitoring Solutions
   - Air Filtration Systems
2. **Products**
   - Biological Safety Cabinets
   - Laminar Flow Clean Benches
   - Laboratory Fume Hoods
   - Autoclaves
   - Laboratory Freezers
   - Centrifuges
   - CO2 Incubators
   - Laboratory Furniture
   - Environmental Monitoring Instruments
3. **Services**
   - Commissioning, Qualification & Validation (CQV)
   - Cleanroom and Air Quality Testing
   - Safety Cabinet and Hood Certification
   - Planned Preventive Maintenance (PPM)
   - Consultancy
   - Installation
   - Training
4. **Projects**
5. **Standards & Credentials**
6. **About**
7. **Contact / Request a Quote**

This also corrects the current product names:

- “Centerfusion” → **Centrifuges**
- “CO2 incupatr” → **CO2 Incubators**
- “Laminar airflow hood” → **Laminar Flow Clean Bench** or **Laminar Flow Cabinet**
- “Fumehoods” → **Laboratory Fume Hoods**
- “Certification CQV” → **Commissioning, Qualification & Validation (CQV)**

## 4. Product-card criteria

Each catalog card should show only the three most useful comparison criteria. Full specifications belong on the product-detail page.

| Product family | Three criteria shown on the card | Important full-page criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Biological Safety Cabinets | Cabinet class/type; working width; exhaust configuration | Protection provided, inflow/downflow velocity, HEPA filtration, alarms, utilities, noise, lighting, applicable certification standard and available test certificate |
| Laminar Flow Clean Benches | Horizontal/vertical flow; working width; cleanliness/filtration rating | Airflow velocity and uniformity, HEPA filter specification, work-zone dimensions, noise, lighting, controls and accessories |
| Laboratory Fume Hoods | Ducted/ductless; working width; required exhaust/face velocity | Sash type, exhaust volume and static pressure, liner/worktop material, chemical compatibility, alarms, controls, service fixtures and applicable performance test |
| Laboratory Freezers | Temperature class; storage capacity; alarm/monitoring features | Temperature range, uniformity, recovery, refrigeration system, refrigerant, shelves/racks, security, remote monitoring, power and dimensions |
| Autoclaves | Chamber capacity; loading format; available cycles | Temperature/pressure range, cycle time, drying, steam source, chamber material, data recording, validation ports, door interlocks and safety features |
| Centrifuges | Maximum RCF; capacity/rotor; refrigerated or non-refrigerated | Speed range, compatible rotors/tubes, temperature range, imbalance detection, acceleration/deceleration profiles, noise, dimensions and power |
| CO2 Incubators | Chamber volume; temperature/CO2 range; contamination-control method | Temperature/CO2 uniformity and recovery, sensor type, humidity method, sterilization cycle, HEPA filtration if fitted, alarms, data logging, shelves and doors |
| Laboratory Furniture | Worktop/material; dimensions/configuration; chemical resistance | Load rating, cleanability, cabinet/storage options, service modules, sink options, mobility, fire resistance where applicable and installation requirements |
| Environmental Monitoring Instruments | Measured parameter; range/accuracy; connectivity/data logging | Sampling flow and particle channels where relevant, sensor locations, alarm logic, audit trail, software, communications protocol, calibration, mapping and reporting |

### Essential safety language

- A **biological safety cabinet (BSC)** is selected according to the protection and exhaust arrangement required. Use Class I, Class II (A1, A2, B1 or B2), and Class III only when supported by the offered model and its documentation.
- A **laminar-flow clean bench** protects the work/product but does **not** protect the operator from hazardous biological material. Do not market it for biohazard work.
- A **fume hood** controls chemical fumes and vapors. It is not a substitute for a biological safety cabinet.
- Do not claim that a product is “NSF certified” merely because Masarat has an NSF-related credential. Product certification, field-certifier credentials, and company accreditation are different claims.

## 5. Product-detail page template

Use the same page structure for every product family:

1. Product name and clear one-line purpose
2. Product image or verified manufacturer image
3. Three headline specifications
4. Overview
5. Applications
6. Available models and variants
7. Full technical-specification table
8. Standards and certification scope
9. Site, utility and installation requirements
10. Options and accessories
11. Installation, commissioning, qualification and training available from Masarat
12. Downloads: brochure, datasheet, manual and certificate where authorized
13. “Request a Quote” form

Until manufacturer/model data is available, publish **product-family pages**, not invented model numbers or specifications. Add individual SKU/model pages only from approved supplier datasheets.

## 7. Solution-page content

### Modular Cleanrooms

Lead with Masarat's modular cleanroom solution. Explain the chemical resistance, smooth impervious finish and durability of the GRP panel system, together with the available finishes, doors, HVAC/mechanical integration and full design-to-commissioning delivery chain. Show relevant products as **components**, not as unrelated catalog items.

### Biocontainment Laboratories

Organize by facility outcome: BSL-3 and BSL-4 design, construction, airflow/pressure control, containment equipment, commissioning, certification, training and ongoing support. Cross-link BSCs, autoclaves, selected freezers, monitoring instruments and suitable furniture.

### Environmental Monitoring Systems

Organize by what is measured: non-viable particles, viable/microbial contamination, temperature, relative humidity and room differential pressure. Explain alarms, trending, reporting, calibration and integration. Cross-link the CQV service because monitoring hardware and facility qualification are complementary.

### Air Filtration Systems

Explain the system as a chain: air handling, terminal filtration, controlled airflow, pressure relationships, exhaust and validation. Cross-link HEPA leak testing and pressure/airflow validation. Only show specific filtration products after the supplied range is confirmed.

## 8. Service-page content

### Commissioning, Qualification & Validation (CQV)

Show three service cards:

- **Cleanroom CQV:** design review, commissioning, classification and performance verification
- **Equipment Qualification:** IQ/OQ/PQ scope, protocols, execution and reports
- **Air Quality & Containment Validation:** airflow, pressure differential, airborne particles, HEPA integrity, smoke visualization and optional microbial/environmental checks

Each page should state the facility/equipment covered, tests included, governing standard or approved protocol, deliverables, prerequisites and recommended requalification interval. Do not promise a universal interval; it depends on the standard, risk assessment and client quality system.

### Safety Cabinet Certification & PPM

Show three service cards:

- **Initial/Relocation Certification**
- **Periodic Re-certification**
- **Planned Preventive Maintenance & Repair**

The BSC certification page can list the supported tests already present in the profile: inflow, downflow, HEPA leak, smoke visualization, alarm/calibration, light intensity, vibration and electrical safety. The service report/certificate, traceable instrument calibration and identified standard should be prominent deliverables.

## 9. NSF credentials PDF placement

Create a **Standards & Credentials** page and add a credential card containing:

- Exact credential title as written on the PDF
- Credential holder (person or company)
- Issuer
- Certificate/credential number, if public
- Scope
- Issue and expiry dates, if shown
- **View NSF Credential (PDF)** button opening the PDF in a new tab

Also place a smaller “View credential” link on the Biological Safety Cabinet Certification page. Do not write “NSF accredited” or apply an NSF claim to every cabinet unless the PDF and the individual product documentation explicitly support that wording. Confirm permission before reproducing certification-body logos.
