Masarat for Accreditation was founded in 2016 by Saudi expertise in the field of
biosafety and cleanrooms, to help clients achieve the highest infection and
contamination control standards and comply with accreditation bodies' requirements
— including SFDA, CBAHI, WHO, CAP, CDC and many others.

We are a company specialized in the **testing, commissioning and validation of
clean rooms and sterile areas**, and we provide **end-to-end solutions**: a wide
range of services from consultancy in biosafety, infection and contamination
control and cleanrooms, to the testing and maintenance of cleanrooms, biosafety
laboratories, controlled environments, biological safety cabinets, horizontal
laminar airflow cabinets, isolators and fume hoods — all to meet international
standards.

Our work is delivered by a team of engineers and technologists, backed by a wide
network of national and international experts — mobilised to match the demands of

- **Intro:** From the first consultation to final certification and training,

**Service cards (8):** *(use the same 8 categories as Home, each linking to detail)*

**Featured service — Air Quality Validation Testing**
Masarat can undertake **Air Quality Validation testing and accreditation to
international standards** (i.e. ISO 14644), and provide consultancy advice — or
even design and construction. Our experienced engineers and technologists help you
maintain operations to internationally approved standards.

*Areas we test:* operating rooms · drug-preparation rooms · isolation rooms ·
sterile areas (CSSD, IVF lab) · other areas requiring sterility or special airflow.

*Tests:* air flow rates (supply & extract) · containment & integrity · HEPA filter
leak · pressure differential · airborne particle counting · smoke visualization.

*Optional:* microbial counts · lighting tests · sound & vibration tests ·
evaluation of gowning and operational protocols.

**Featured service — Biological Safety Cabinet Testing & Certification**
Certifying your cabinets provides safety for **patients/products, personnel and
the environment**, and meets the requirements of accreditation bodies (e.g. JCIA,
CBAHI).

*Tests:* inflow air velocity · downflow air velocity · HEPA filter leak · smoke
visualization · site installation · light intensity · vibration · electrical
safety.
