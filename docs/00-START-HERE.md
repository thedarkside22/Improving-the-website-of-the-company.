# 00 — Start Here: Your Learning Path

Welcome. This is the map. Follow it in order. Each doc is short and practical.

## The mindset shift

You are not *building a new site*. You are **maintaining a living one** that
real customers visit right now. That changes everything:

- A bug isn't "my local app looks wrong" — it's "a potential client saw a
  broken page and left."
- You rarely start from a blank file. You **read first, change second**.
- The most valuable skill is **not** writing code fast. It's **changing things
  safely and being able to undo**.

If you internalize one idea from all of this: **always have a way back.**

## The path

Read these in order. Budget ~2–3 focused hours total for a first pass.

1. **[01 — How the site is built](01-how-the-site-is-built.md)**
   What we know about m4acc.com's stack and how we know it. (WordPress.)

2. **[02 — WordPress fundamentals](02-wordpress-fundamentals.md)**
   The mental model: how WordPress turns a request into a page; where content,
   design, and behavior live (database, theme, plugins).

3. **[03 — Staging explained](03-staging-explained.md)** ← *the core lesson*
   What staging is, why it exists, the three environments, and the full
   workflow. This is the heart of what you asked for.

4. **[04 — Safe editing playbook](04-safe-editing-playbook.md)**
   The concrete "do this / never do that" rules for changing a WordPress site
   without taking it down.

5. **[05 — Backups and recovery](05-backups-and-recovery.md)**
   How to back up, and — more importantly — how to *restore* when something
   breaks.

6. **[06 — Local development setup](06-local-development-setup.md)**
   Build a private copy of the site on your own computer to experiment freely.

7. **[07 — Deployment and promotion](07-deployment-and-promotion.md)**
   How a tested change travels from staging to production safely.

8. **[08 — Discovery checklist](08-discovery-checklist.md)**
   The 10-minute investigation to learn the things we couldn't see from
   outside: exact theme, plugins, host, and backup status.

9. **[09 — Glossary](09-glossary.md)**
   Every term you'll meet, in plain English. Keep it open in a tab.

## Then measure yourself

- **[Self-assessment quiz](../assessments/01-self-assessment-quiz.md)** — take
  it *before* you read deeply (to find your baseline) and *after* (to see
  growth).
- **[Hands-on tasks](../assessments/02-hands-on-tasks.md)** — do these on a
  local/staging copy to turn reading into skill.
- **[Answer key & rubric](../assessments/03-answer-key-and-rubric.md)** — check
  yourself and find your level band.

## What you should be able to say when you're done

- "I can explain the difference between local, staging, and production, and why
  each exists."
- "I can spin up a copy of the site and break it on purpose, then restore it."
- "I know the handful of actions that can take a WordPress site down, and I
  avoid them on production."
- "Before any production change, I take a backup and I have a rollback plan."

That's the bar. Let's get you there.
