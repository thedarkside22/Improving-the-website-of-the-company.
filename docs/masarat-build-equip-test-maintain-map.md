# Masarat Solutions Pathway Map

## Purpose

This structure organizes Masarat's offering into three clear client pathways:

- **Build**: for clients who need to create or upgrade a controlled environment.
- **Equip**: for clients who need laboratory, cleanroom, or safety products.
- **Test & Maintain**: for clients who need validation, certification, maintenance, or ongoing performance support.

The goal is to keep the first website experience simple while still allowing clients to go deeper into the right solution, product, or service page.

## Notes Before Publishing

- Confirm whether Masarat supplies ULPA filters, fan filter units, microbial air samplers, ductless fume hoods, and high-containment exhaust systems before listing them as standalone products.
- If an item is not confirmed as a direct product, describe it as part of an engineered solution instead.
- Do not present laminar flow clean benches as biological safety cabinets.
- Do not claim a product is certified unless the specific product documentation supports that claim.
- Keep the first Solutions page simple. Use the detailed pages to explain full technical scope.
